# E8 — Factor-Flip Study (RQ3, core)

Causal, not correlational. For each candidate factor, a **minimal pair** (same
underlying quantity, flip one axis) → effect = ΔF1(on) − ΔF1(off), mean±std over
≥5 seeds. A factor enters the rule only if flipping it flips help↔no-help.

Factors: F1 input-varying, F2 stochastic, F3 label-driven, F4 redundant-with-aggregation.
Plus a 2×2 (input-variation × redundancy) to test AND-gate vs additive.
R² + loss-plateau reported as supporting covariates (checked *within* matched pairs).
