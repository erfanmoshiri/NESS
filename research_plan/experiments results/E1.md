## Reading

- **On ogbn-arxiv (dense, large), NESS wins clearly:** F1 0.454 vs FP 0.439, MATE 0.404,
  PaGCN 0.383 (+0.015 over the best baseline, +0.05 over MATE); accuracy likewise highest
  (0.674). This is the headline result and the regime the paper targets.
- **NESS is also best or tied on citeseer (0.735) and amac (0.911)**, and competitive on
  cora and amap. On the small binary datasets the strong methods (FP, PaGCN, MATE) are all
  close, so margins are narrow; the clear separation appears on the large dense graph.

## Framing for the paper

Frame E1 as: NESS is best or competitive across datasets, with a clear win in the target
regime (large, dense-embedding ogbn-arxiv); on small binary benchmarks strong methods
converge and margins are narrow. Ties to E9 (feature-type boundary) and E4 (advantage
grows with missingness).
