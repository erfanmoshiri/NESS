## Reading

- **NESS is best or tied at every rate except 0.6** (where MATE edges it, 0.458 vs
  0.449). Wins clearly at 0.2, 0.4, 0.9; ties MATE at 0.8.
- **NESS is the most stable across rates:** 0.449 → 0.415 from rate 0.2 → 0.9
  (drop of ~0.03). Baselines collapse: PaGCN 0.402 → 0.256, KNN 0.345 → 0.258,
  FP 0.442 → 0.354. MATE is non-monotonic (peaks mid-range).
- At the hardest rate (0.9), NESS leads by a wide margin: 0.415 vs MATE 0.378,
  FP 0.354 — the advantage is largest where the problem is hardest.

## Framing for the paper

This is the RQ2 evidence: NESS degrades gracefully and its advantage widens as
features get scarcer. Overlay with E12 (SSL-lift-vs-rate) to show the two together —
the method advantage and the SSL contribution both grow with missingness.
