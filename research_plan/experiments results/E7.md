# E7 — View Analysis (RQ4)

Vary the second contrastive view, objective fixed to hist+path. Which view best
supports the shared representation, and why. arxiv MCAR @ 80%, SAGE-128, 800 epochs.

**Source:** `experiments/e7_view_ablation.sh` → `ogbn_benchmarks/results/e7_viewablation_20260723_112905/`.

**Status:** DONE (single-seed). Multi-seed (E2) still needed — gaps are ~0.01–0.02.

## Findings for the paper

1. **Complexity ≠ quality — the simplest view wins.** edge_mask (simplest, 0.394) is
   top; the elaborate views (deep 0.371, feat_mask 0.353) are the worst. Richer view
   construction does not help under severe missingness.

2. **FP-prefilled features are at least as good as PPR diffusion for the 2nd view.**
   edge_mask (both views on FP-prefilled features, 0.394) ≈ ppr (2nd view = PPR-of-raw,
   0.392) — a 0.002 gap, within noise. The PPR diffusion machinery buys nothing over
   the prefill we already do. (Honest phrasing: prefill ≥ ppr, not strictly "better".)

3. **Structure-perturbing views > feature-perturbing views.** The top two (edge_mask,
   ppr) both perturb *structure*; the feature-perturbing view (feat_mask) is worst. This
   is the **same axis as E3** — structure-based signal helps, feature-based doesn't — now
   confirmed on the *view* axis too.

4. **Stochasticity is orthogonal (no relation).** The best view (edge_mask) is stochastic
   and the 2nd-best (ppr) is static; the worst (feat_mask) is stochastic. Static/stochastic
   does not separate good views from bad — same conclusion as E3's objectives.

5. **The view axis is low-impact overall.** Consistent with E3 (single_view = no_ssl):
   the contrastive term the second view feeds contributes ~0 at 80% missing, so *which*
   view is chosen barely moves downstream F1.

## Takeaways
- **Use edge_mask** (the default) — simplest and best; drop the PPR complexity.
- RQ4 is largely a **negative result**: view construction is not a meaningful lever under
  severe missingness. Pairs with the RQ3 structure-vs-feature finding.
- Consider a leaner NESS ablation (drop the 2nd view / contrastive entirely) — both E3 and
  E7 suggest the contrastive machinery is inert here.

**Caveat:** single-seed; ~0.01–0.02 gaps within plausible noise. E2 required before firm claims.
